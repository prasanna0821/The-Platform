# Vistaar / The Platform

The Vistaar film and The Platform deck for the banca platform, LRS FY27-30. GitHub does not accept files over 100 MB inside a repository, so the film and the deck are attached to releases.

**Latest release, the film and the deck together:** https://github.com/prasanna0821/The-Platform/releases/latest

## The film: Vistaar v104

2:58.5, 1080p, with sound. A proposed FY30 experience with fictional customers and illustrative outcomes, in eight chapters: opening, business to win, business to keep (the renewal book and the call, Ananya's call, the Marathi video), business to open up, the close, and dusk. Rendered 23 September 2026.

## The deck: The Platform

The deck as one self-contained HTML file, `The_Platform_Standalone.html` (34 slides, four films embedded, 17 September 2026 edition). Every stylesheet, script, font, illustration, app capture and film is embedded, so it opens and plays from any folder with nothing else beside it. Open it in Chrome or Edge: arrow keys move between slides, N opens the speaker notes, the slide counter opens the index.

The ten-slide LRS outcomes run, `Vistaar_LRS_Outcomes_11.html`, is on its own release: https://github.com/prasanna0821/The-Platform/releases/tag/deck-2026-09-23
